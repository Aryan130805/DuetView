ENGINEERED EXCELLENCE

Designed for Performance & Reliability

Every layer of DuetView is tuned for high responsiveness, crystal-clear clarity, and rock-solid local peer-to-peer security.

Wireless

High-Speed Wi-Fi Streaming

Connect untethered across your local 5 GHz or 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Adaptive bitrates ensure uninterrupted streaming even with fluctuating signal quality.

Wired USB

Ultra-Low Latency USB Tethering

Plug in a USB cable and activate USB tethering for sub-5ms glass latency, zero Wi-Fi interference, and maximum 12,000 Kbps lossless visual fidelity.

IddCx Driver

Virtual Extended Display

A genuine virtual secondary display driver integrated into Windows. Drag apps, organize workspaces, and position monitors freely in Windows Settings.

Zero Driver

Driverless Desktop Mirroring

Capture and duplicate your desktop instantly with Microsoft DXGI Desktop Duplication without needing driver installation or system reboots.

GPU Hardware

Hardware-Accelerated Codec

H.264 video encoding utilizing NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, and Intel QuickSync, paired with Android MediaCodec hardware decoding for minimal battery drain.

Touch & Pen

Interactive Touch & Input

Control Windows directly from your Android touchscreen with left-click, right-click, tap-to-click, fluid dragging, and precision scrolling gesture recognition.

Instant Pair

QR Code & Auto-Discovery

Forget typing IP addresses. DuetView discovers PCs on the local network automatically or pairs instantly with a one-second QR code scan.

Settings

Custom Display Preferences

Fine-tune frame rate targets (30, 60, 120 FPS), bitrate profiles (Fast, Balanced, Lossless), custom display aspect ratios, and touch presets.

QUICK START

Up and Running in 4 Simple Steps

From download to a live dual-monitor workflow in under two minutes. No account creation or cloud setups required.

1

Install DuetView PC

Download and run the installer on your Windows computer. The desktop host service starts automatically on your local network.

2

Install on Android

Download and install the companion APK on your Android smartphone or tablet. Grant local network and camera permissions.

3

Connect via Wi-Fi or USB

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi router or connect a USB data cable with Android USB tethering enabled.

4

Pair & Start Streaming

Scan the on-screen QR code or select your PC from the discovered list. Your Android device instantly displays your Windows desktop!

DUAL MODES

Virtual Extended Screen vs. Mirroring

Choose the right mode for your workflow: expand your Windows desktop workspace or duplicate your screen with zero driver setup.

Extended Desktop

Virtual Extended Display

Powered by the Microsoft Indirect Display Driver (IddCx), DuetView creates an authentic, independent second screen in Windows Display Settings. Drag windows across displays freely.

  • Recognized by Windows as a real independent monitor
  • Drag apps, browser tabs, and code editors across screens
  • Custom resolutions up to 2560x1600 matching your tablet
Plug & Play

Desktop Mirroring

Need an instant second screen without installing kernel or indirect drivers? DuetView captures your desktop with DirectX DXGI Desktop Duplication for zero-configuration mirroring.

  • Zero driver installation or administrator setup required
  • Full touch digitizer input injected directly into Windows
  • Ideal for presentations, portable media, and quick checks

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Have questions regarding compatibility, wireless setup, or driver installation? Find quick answers below.

DuetView captures desktop frames via Microsoft's DirectX Graphics Infrastructure (DXGI) Desktop Duplication API. Frames are encoded in real time using dedicated GPU silicon (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, or Intel QuickSync) and transmitted over a lightweight, zero-copy local socket directly to your Android device, where they are decoded by hardware via Android's MediaCodec API.
No. Never. DuetView is strictly a 100% peer-to-peer local application. It operates entirely over your local Wi-Fi router or a direct USB tethering cable. Your desktop video feed, touch events, and session credentials never leave your local devices and are never transmitted to any external server.
Virtual Extended Display installs an Indirect Display Driver (IddCx) so Windows recognizes an independent second monitor, allowing you to drag windows and organize displays. Desktop Mirroring requires zero drivers; it duplicates your existing primary screen onto your Android tablet for presentations or touch control.
Yes! Plug your Android device into your Windows PC with a USB cable and enable USB Tethering in Android Settings. DuetView automatically detects the USB network adapter and routes all video streaming over the high-speed wired interface.
DuetView supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) with any modern Intel, AMD, or NVIDIA GPU supporting DXGI 1.2+ and H.264 video hardware encoding. On mobile, DuetView supports Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) through Android 15 on both phones and tablets.
