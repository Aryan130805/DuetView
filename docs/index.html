DUAL MODES

Built for Real Multitasking Power

Whether you want a genuine secondary desktop workspace to drag windows into or an instant mirroring canvas with touch interaction, DuetView adapts to your workflow.

Flagship Mode

Virtual Extended Display

Powered by the high-performance Windows Indirect Display Driver (IddCx), DuetView creates an authentic, native secondary monitor recognized directly by Windows Display Settings.

  • Drag apps, code editors, and browsers across monitors
  • Independent resolution scaling (1080p, 2K, tablet native)
  • Supports landscape & portrait device orientations
Zero Driver Setup

Desktop Mirroring

Need an instant second display without installing kernel or indirect drivers? DuetView features a driver-free Desktop Mirroring mode leveraging direct Windows DXGI desktop duplication.

  • Zero installation hurdles or administrator driver prompts
  • Full touch input with direct Windows mouse injection
  • Ideal for presentations, media viewing, and on-the-go setups

QUICK START ROADMAP

Up and Running in 5 Simple Steps

From download to a live dual-monitor workflow in under two minutes. No account creation or cloud setups required.

1

Download & Install

Download the DuetView PC setup for your Windows computer and the DuetView Android APK for your tablet or phone from our official release channels.

2

Launch DuetView PC

Start DuetView PC on Windows. The background capture engine initializes and listens securely on your local network (ports 52025/52026).

3

Open DuetView on Android

Open the companion app on your Android device. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi router or connect a USB-C cable with tethering enabled.

4

Pair via QR or Auto-Discovery

Tap your discovered PC in the device list or scan the displayed QR code on your PC screen with your Android camera for instant pairing.

5

Enjoy Your Second Screen

Your Android device instantly becomes a buttery-smooth 60 FPS monitor. Drag windows, control apps with touch gestures, and boost your daily productivity!

Quick Installation Instructions

Android

On your Android device, tap the downloaded APK. If prompted, grant permission to "Install unknown apps" for your browser. Once installed, grant Camera (for QR code pairing) and Local Network permissions.

Windows

Run DuetView-PC-Setup.exe. When Windows Defender SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway. When prompted by Windows Firewall on first run, check Private networks to allow streaming.

Extended Mode

To use the full Virtual Extended Display mode, run install_driver.bat from the Drivers tab in DuetView PC to install the Microsoft-signed IddCx virtual monitor driver.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about network setup, driver requirements, or compatibility? Find fast answers below.

DuetView uses a high-performance native C++ streaming pipeline that captures desktop frames via Microsoft's DirectX Graphics Infrastructure (DXGI) Desktop Duplication API. Frames are encoded in real time using dedicated GPU silicon (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, or Intel QuickSync) and transmitted over a lightweight, zero-copy TCP/UDP socket directly to the Android device, where they are decoded by hardware via Android's MediaCodec API.

No. Never. DuetView is strictly a 100% peer-to-peer local application. It operates entirely over your local Wi-Fi router or a direct USB tethering cable. Your desktop video feed, touch events, and session credentials never leave your local physical devices and are never transmitted to any external server.

Virtual Extended Display installs an Indirect Display Driver (IddCx) that makes Windows see a genuine second monitor. You can drag independent windows, arrange display positions, and multitask across screens. Desktop Mirroring requires zero drivers or administrator setup; it instantly duplicates your existing primary screen onto your Android tablet for quick presentations or remote touch control.

In DuetView PC, navigate to the Driver / Extended Display tab. Click Install Virtual Display Driver. Windows will install the Microsoft-compliant virtual display adapter. Once installed, your Android device will appear as Display 2 in Windows Settings → System → Display.

Yes! Simply plug your Android device into your Windows PC with a USB-C or Micro-USB cable and enable USB Tethering in Android Settings. DuetView automatically detects the USB network adapter and routes all video streaming over the high-speed wired interface at maximum bitrate.

DuetView supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) with any modern Intel, AMD, or NVIDIA GPU supporting DXGI 1.2+ and H.264 video hardware encoding. On mobile, DuetView supports Android 8.0 (Oreo, API level 26) through Android 15 on both phones and tablets.

DuetView PC hosts a local peer-to-peer TCP/UDP streaming socket on port 52025 and auto-discovery broadcasts on port 52026. Windows Firewall prompts you to ensure you want to allow incoming connections from your Android device on your private local network.
