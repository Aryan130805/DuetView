DuetView Privacy Policy

Your privacy is absolute. DuetView is engineered with a strict zero-cloud, 100% peer-to-peer architecture.

01 Introduction & Philosophy

DuetView is developed by Aryan (@Aryan130805) as an open-source, high-performance companion display system that turns your Android phone or tablet into an extended monitor or mirror for your Windows 10/11 computer.

We believe that desktop screen mirroring and display extension should never compromise user privacy. This document outlines how our Android application and Windows host software handle device permissions and network communication.

02 Zero Cloud & Peer-to-Peer Architecture

DuetView operates entirely as a peer-to-peer (P2P) system:

  • Direct Connection: Your Android client establishes a direct TCP socket connection (port 52025) and UDP discovery channel (port 52026) directly to your Windows host machine.
  • No Intermediary Relays: Video frames, touch gestures, and keyboard inputs flow directly between your phone and computer over your private Wi-Fi network or USB cable.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate, rent, or maintain any relay servers, database clusters, or external proxy services. None of your screen content ever traverses the internet.

03 Information We Collect

We collect NO personal data.

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of DuetView without registering, logging in, or providing an email address.
  • No Device Profiling: We do not collect device serial numbers, IMEI, advertising IDs (GAID), or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Usage Tracking: We do not track session durations, opened screens, or user interactions.

04 Device Permissions & How They Are Used

DuetView requests only the minimum required Android permissions, strictly utilized for local operational purposes:

📷 Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)
Purpose: Used exclusively to scan the pairing QR code displayed on your Windows PC monitor.
Data Handling: Camera frames are processed strictly in real-time on your device using on-device ML/ZXing barcode recognition. Camera frames are never saved, recorded, or transmitted. Camera access terminates the instant the QR code is decoded. Manual IP entry is available if you prefer not to grant camera access.
🌐 Local Network & Wi-Fi State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE)
Purpose: Required to enumerate local network interfaces, bind local TCP sockets, and stream display frames between your Android device and Windows PC across local Wi-Fi or USB tethering.
📡 Wi-Fi Multicast (CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE)
Purpose: Allows the Android application to listen for UDP discovery beacons broadcast by your Windows host on your local network so your PC appears automatically in the available devices list.

05 Local Data Storage

DuetView stores only minimal, non-sensitive preferences directly on your device via standard private Android SharedPreferences:

  • Saved PC Profiles: Names, local IP addresses, and pairing public keys of previously paired computers so you can reconnect with one tap.
  • Display Preferences: Your selected resolution, target refresh rate (60/90/120 FPS), and display scaling preferences.

This information is private to the application sandbox and is permanently erased when you clear app data or uninstall DuetView.

06 Third-Party Services & Advertising

DuetView contains:

  • Zero Advertising Networks: No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or programmatic advertising SDKs.
  • Zero Analytics Platforms: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK.
  • Zero Data Brokers: No selling, renting, or monetization of user data whatsoever.

07 Children's Privacy

DuetView does not address anyone under the age of 13 and complies fully with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play Families Policy. Because we collect zero personal data, children can safely use DuetView as a secondary display without privacy risk.
