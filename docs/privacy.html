Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy for the DuetView Android companion and Windows PC application.

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Application: DuetView (Android & Windows)

Core Privacy Guarantee: Zero Cloud • 100% Peer-to-Peer

DuetView operates strictly across your private local Wi-Fi network or direct USB cable. We do not operate cloud servers, and we collect zero personal data, analytics, or screen content. Everything stays on your devices.

1 Overview & Architecture

DuetView is developed by Aryan (@Aryan130805) as an open-source productivity app that converts your Android device into an extended monitor or mirror for your Windows PC.

Unlike conventional remote desktop applications that route video streams through third-party cloud infrastructure, DuetView is engineered as a direct peer-to-peer (P2P) local network protocol. Video encoding on Windows and hardware decoding on Android communicate directly over your home/office LAN or USB cable.

2 Information We Collect

DuetView collects zero personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Device Identifiers: No IMEI, MAC addresses, Android IDs, or advertising identifiers (GAID).
  • No Screen Content Recording: Screen frames generated by your Windows PC are decoded directly to the Android hardware SurfaceView in memory and are never saved or uploaded.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Tracking: Zero analytics SDKs, session loggers, or crash-reporting trackers.

3 Device Permissions Explained

DuetView requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for local operation:

📷 Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)
Usage: Used strictly to scan the pairing QR code displayed by the DuetView Windows PC host.
Data Handling: Camera frames are processed purely in real-time on your device using on-device barcode scanning. Images are never stored, recorded, or transmitted. You can bypass camera permission entirely by manually entering your PC's IP address.
🌐 Local Network (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE)
Usage: Required to enumerate your local network interface (Wi-Fi or USB tethering) and open a direct local TCP socket (port 52025) to stream video and input controls between your PC and phone.
📡 Wi-Fi Multicast (CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE)
Usage: Allows the Android client to receive UDP discovery beacons broadcast by your Windows PC on your local subnet (port 52026) so your computer is automatically discovered.

4 Local On-Device Storage

DuetView saves only basic, non-sensitive preferences locally on your phone using standard Android private storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Saved PC Profiles: Hostnames, local IP addresses, and pairing public keys of previously connected PCs for convenient one-tap reconnection.
  • Display Preferences: Your preferred resolution mode, target refresh rate (60/90/120 FPS), and scaling preference.

This data never leaves your device and is wiped immediately if you clear app storage or uninstall the application.

5 Third-Party Services & Advertising

DuetView contains:

  • Zero Advertisements: No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or programmatic advertising SDKs.
  • Zero Analytics: No Firebase, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK.
  • Zero Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not share, sell, or rent any data to any third party.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

DuetView does not target or collect data from children under the age of 13. Because our software does not collect any personal data whatsoever, DuetView is safe for all audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
